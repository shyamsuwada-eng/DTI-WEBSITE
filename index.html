Who We Are

About ECE Department

The ECE Department at Lendi Institute of Engineering and Technology is dedicated to providing a solid foundation in electronics and communication engineering. Our comprehensive curriculum combines theoretical knowledge with practical skills through symposiums, workshops, and industry collaboration.

Online courses, seminars, and webinars led by industry experts ensure our students stay updated with the latest advancements. Through technical assessments, mock interviews, and career guidance, our graduates have secured positions in top MNCs with impressive packages.

At Lendi's ECE Department, student growth and knowledge acquisition are paramount.
